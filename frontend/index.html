Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my order? +
Orders are paid before dispatch via secure online payment (cards, UPI, netbanking) through Razorpay. Once payment is confirmed, we begin packing and shipping your order.
How long does delivery take? +
Most orders are dispatched within 24-48 hours and arrive in 3-6 business days depending on your location. Track your order anytime with your Order ID.
Do you deliver crackers safely? +
Every order is packed as per transport safety norms, moisture-sealed and boxed to prevent damage in transit.
Is there a minimum order for free shipping? +
Orders above ₹2,000 get free shipping. Below that, a flat ₹150 shipping fee applies.
Can I buy in bulk for events? +
Yes — reach out via the contact section below for wholesale and bulk-order pricing on large quantities.
